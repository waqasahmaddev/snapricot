Snap it. Mark it. Share it.

A fast, free, open-source screenshot tool. Capture an area, a window, or the whole screen with one hotkey, and it is on your clipboard and saved to disk before you blink.

Download for Windows See features

Windows 10 / 11, 64-bit · about 16 MB · MIT licensed

Capture exactly what you mean

Three ways to capture, each on its own hotkey. Every hotkey can be changed.

Custom area

Drag to select, then fine-tune with eight handles, arrow-key nudging, an exact width and height, or an aspect-ratio preset.

Active window

One keystroke captures the window you are working in, without the clutter around it.

Full screen

Grab the whole display. The screen freezes first, so what you see is what you get.

Built to stay out of your way

Snapricot lives in the tray and is ready when you press the key.

Copy and save in one step

Copy puts the image on your clipboard and saves it to your folder. Prefer to choose a name and place? Use Save.

Light on resources

Compiled ahead of time with Native AOT. Targets: start in under 300 ms and idle under 60 MB.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no telemetry. Your captures never leave your machine unless you share them.

Make it yours

Rebind hotkeys, pick PNG or JPG, choose where notifications appear, and start with Windows if you like.

What is coming

Snapricot is in early development. Capture works today; the rest is on the roadmap.
