Settings

Open Settings from the sidebar. Your choices are stored in %APPDATA%\Snapricot\settings.json.

General

  • Start with Windows. Launches Snapricot at sign-in, opening in the tray.
  • Notification position. Bottom right (default), bottom left, top right or top left.
  • Capture sound. An optional shutter sound.

Save to

  • Default folder for captures.
  • Format: PNG or JPG.
  • Open folder after saving.

Hotkeys

Record a new combination for each action, see conflict warnings, and reset to defaults. Details on the Hotkeys page.

Reset everything

Close Snapricot and delete settings.json. It is recreated with defaults on the next start.
