Getting started

1. Install

Download the zip from the Releases page, extract it, and run Snapricot.exe. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway (see Download).

2. Take a capture

Press Ctrl+PrtSc from anywhere to capture a custom area. You can also click a capture tile on the Snapricot home screen, or use the tray icon menu.

  1. The screen freezes and the selection overlay appears.
  2. Drag to choose an area. Adjust it with the handles, the arrow keys, or by typing an exact size.
  3. Click Copy to put it on the clipboard (it is also saved to your folder), Save to choose a name and folder, or Cancel.

3. Find your capture

By default captures are saved in Pictures\Snapricot. The home screen shows your most recent captures, and a notification appears after each capture.

Other capture modes

  • Alt+PrtSc captures the active window.
  • Shift+PrtSc captures the full screen.

No Print Screen key on your laptop? Use Ctrl+Shift+1, 2 or 3. See Hotkeys.
