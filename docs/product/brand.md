## Name

**Snapricot** = *snap* + *apricot*. Pronounced **SNAP-ri-kot** (/ˈsnæp.rɪ.kɒt/). Always written as one word with a capital S.

## Logo

**Concept:** an apricot with one slice snapped out and held inside a dashed selection frame. The slice is "the piece
of the screen you captured." It's a fruit, not a tile: there's deliberately no square background and no corner
brackets, so it stays clearly distinct from other capture tools (e.g. Icecream Screen Recorder's orange square).

PNGs are generated by `pwsh tools/brand/Render-Logo.ps1`. If you change the SVG, mirror the geometry in the script
and re-run it.

**Rules:** keep clear space of at least 1/4 of the logo width around it. Don't put it in a tile, recolor it, add effects,
or rotate it. On busy photos, place it on a Plum or white card.

## Colors

| Swatch | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🟠 | **Apricot** | `#F4943C` | Logo fruit, illustrations, marketing highlights |
| 🟧 | **Apricot deep** | `#D9622B` | **UI accent**: primary button (Copy), selection border, active tool, focus, links; logo seam and frame |
| 🍑 | **Flesh** | `#FFC98F` | Logo slice; accent text on dark tints |
| 🟢 | **Leaf** | `#3E9B6A` | Logo leaf; success states |
| 🟤 | **Stem** | `#6B4A2B` | Logo stem only |
| 🟣 | **Plum** | `#2E2240` | Primary text and icons in the app; dark brand surfaces (website footer, social images) |

App surfaces: the app uses a **light theme**, white with apricot for anything active or selected (decided 2026-10-09):

| Key | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `ShellBackgroundBrush` | `#FFFFFF` | Main content area |
| `ShellSidebarBrush` | `#FCF8F4` | Sidebar (warm off-white) |
| `ShellBorderBrush` | `#F0E6DC` | Sidebar edge, dividers |
| `ShellMutedTextBrush` | `#8A7F76` | Version label, captions |
| Selected nav item | `BrandApricotDeepBrush` fill + white text | `ListBox.nav` style in `App.axaml` |
| Hover nav item | `BrandTintBrush` fill + `BrandTintTextBrush` text | `ListBox.nav` style in `App.axaml` |

UI tints (theme-aware, in `App.axaml`):

| Key | Light | Dark | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `BrandTintBrush` | `#FFF1E4` | `#4A2A1C` | Shortcut badges, active tool background |
| `BrandTintTextBrush` | `#9A3F14` | `#FFC98F` | Text on `BrandTintBrush` |

Accessibility: white text on Apricot deep has a contrast ratio of about 3.7:1, which passes WCAG AA for large or bold text
and UI components. Use it for buttons with medium or bold labels, and never for small body text. On light backgrounds,
use `#9A3F14` for accent-colored text.

In code, always use the resource keys (`BrandApricotBrush`, `BrandApricotDeepBrush`, `BrandPlumBrush`,
`BrandTintBrush`, `BrandTintTextBrush`), never raw hex values.

## Voice

Plain, friendly, short. Sentence case. Verbs on buttons ("Copy", "Save", "Capture region"). No exclamation marks in
the UI, and no "successfully".

## Tagline

**Snap it. Mark it. Share it.**
